# Imperial squadron review: TIE Fighter aces

**Source:** https://cannotgetyourshipout.blogspot.com/2017/01/imperial-squadron-review-tie-fighter.html
**Date:** 2020-12-08T17:17:00-06:00
**Category:** squadrons
**Tags:** empire

---

- Gains Counter 1
- Gains Escort
- Loses Swarm
- Is Howlrunner at distance 1 of a friendly squadron?
- Does that squadron have the Swarm keyword, regardless of whether or not Swarm is being used?
- Is that squadron attacking another squadron?
- Mauler Mithel needs to complete a movement. This means he will not trigger on placement (such as if he is overlapped by a ship or picked up and dropped by Hondo) and he will not trigger unless he is able to move and chooses to do so (even if he just flies around doing space-donuts and lands in exactly the same place).
- He deals damage only to foes engaged with him after the movement.This means that enemy squadrons that are obstructed to him (hiding 
behind ships, hiding in obstacles) after he ends his movement are not 
damaged, as he is not engaging them.
