# Chicago Worlds guide addendum: Schaumburg

**Source:** https://cannotgetyourshipout.blogspot.com/2018/11/chicago-worlds-guide-addendum-schaumburg.html
**Date:** 2018-11-28T10:48:00-06:00
**Category:** tactics
**Tags:** 

---

- Legoland Discovery Center- This is a kid-friendly attraction with lots of Lego activities.
- Woodfield Mall- This was once the largest mall in the world by store area. It still is a gigantic mall with tons of shops and restaurants.
- Forest Preserves- If you’re looking to enjoy nature, there are a ton of forest preserves in and around Schaumburg.
- Busse Woods is quite nice and has great hiking/biking trails. It also has a herd of Elk that like to come right up to the fence at the corner of Arlington Heights Rd and Higgins Rd.
- Chef Ping- Best Chinese food in the suburbs. Lots of traditional and authentic dishes.1755 W Algonquin Rd, Rolling Meadows, IL 60008
- Naxos: A Greek Island Restaurant- Traditional Greek food with flaming Saganaki, Pastichio, Gyros, Greek Chicken and more. 933 W Irving Park Rd, Itasca, IL 60143
- Sushi Station- Conveyor belt sushi bar. Fun and reasonably priced. 1641 W Algonquin Rd, Rolling Meadows, IL 60008
- Kuma’s Corner- Heavy metal burger bar. Large beer selection and gigantic burgers topped with crazy toppings. (Chicken and waffles on a burger!) This place will be jammed pretty much non-stop during the convention, so expect a long wait. 1570 E Golf Rd, Schaumburg, IL 60173
- Johnnie’s Beef- This place is better than the usual suspects for Italian Beef. They also have Italian Lemonade. 1935 S Arlington Heights Rd, Arlington Heights, IL 60005
- Lou Malnati’s- Best place for deep dish pizza. Locations in Schaumburg and Elk Grove, both are equidistant from the convention center. 1050 E Higgins Rd, Elk Grove Village, IL 60007 or 1 S Roselle Rd, Schaumburg, IL 60193
- Texas de Brazil- Brazilian Steakhouse, guys bring meat on a sword to your table until you say stop. A little pricey for dinner ($50+) but more reasonable at lunchtime.
- Bigby’s Pour House- Craft beer bar with an excellent selection. They don’t have an extensive food menu, so I’d recommend eating before going to this place. 1649 Algonquin Rd, Rolling Meadows, IL 60008 or 1700 W Lake St, Addison, IL 60101
- Village Tavern Grill- Sports bar with great food. Legendary chicken fingers. 901 W Wise Rd Schaumburg, IL 60193
- Bulldog Ale house- A pub with an extensive craft beer list, and great wings. 1480 Golf Rd Rolling Meadows, IL 60008
