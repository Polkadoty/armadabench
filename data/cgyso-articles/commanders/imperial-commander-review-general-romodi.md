# Imperial commander review: General Romodi

**Source:** https://cannotgetyourshipout.blogspot.com/2020/01/imperial-commander-review-general-romodi.html
**Date:** 2021-01-02T17:28:00-06:00
**Category:** commanders
**Tags:** Imperial, empire

---

- General Romodi only works on ship-on-ship attacks.
- His effect will trigger on any amount of obstruction provided at least one source of obstruction was a ship or obstacle.
- This means if your opponent's ship had obstruction from Early Warning System and General Cracken, Romodi is still obstructed like normal and has to drop one die. If it had obstruction from those two sources and an obstacle, then Romodi kicks in instead.
- Remember that when Romodi works, you don't drop a die from obstruction and instead add 1 red die.
- Adding the die is optional if for some reason you don't want to.
- Because the die is added, it happens after your initial roll during the Resolve Attack Effects step.
- If Romodi was worded more like Commander Sato, it would be part of your initial roll, but he's not.
