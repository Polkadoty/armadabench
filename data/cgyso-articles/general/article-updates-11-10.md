- Clarified that Infested Fields does not let squadrons deployed overlapping obstacles pick up the objective tokens. They need to end a move overlapping an obstacle.
- Mentioned that the extra maneuver from Rift Ambush can, in fact, allow you to trigger Fighter Coordination Team twice in one round (for extra stupid fun an Officer Ozzel FCT ship could trigger 3 times in the first round in Rift Ambush under ideal circumstances).
- Mentioned that Hyperspace Migration has the noted downside that SSDs love tight pockets of ships all in one place going whale watching.
- Updated the Arquitens article(again!), specifically the defensive retrofits section. Broke down my top 3 choices and specifically added Advanced Projectors. Ian Cross used Advanced Projector Arqs quite well in the game we had at the tournament. I'm still a little skeptical of equipping Arqs with defensive retrofits by default(I want them as cheap as I can get them usually), but in his fleet (3 Arqs, 1 Gozanti, a blob of Rogues) he needed his Arqs to stick around as long as possible and Advanced Projectors did solid work for him.
- Gave the Raider article an overhaul, which included:
- Standard adding in hyperlinks where appropriate, cleaning up some text here and there.
- Shortened sections that are effectively repeated in the "how to black dice" article and linked to said article.
- Mentioned the dangers of long-ranged snipe specialists and large quantities of squadrons when you can't respond and provided tips on what to do about those.
- Upgrades format standardized and updated.
- Discussed Versio a bit more.
- Added Reserve Hangar Decks and Advanced Transponder Network and Darth Vader boarding team (which I really should've added earlier, but I digress).
- Builds toggled a bit, especially the Raider-I. Dropped two antiquated builds, added a new one (Vader Raider, should've been there earlier).
- Raider-II section cleaned up and elaborated more on how it's used differently from a Raider-I.
- Removed the "Raider friends commanders" section. I'll be adding "ships this commander likes" to all the updated (we're getting there eventually) Imperial commander articles after they're all removed from the Imperial ship articles.
