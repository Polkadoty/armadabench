# C-ROC CIS Gozanti

**Source:** https://cannotgetyourshipout.blogspot.com/2022/09/geek-again-in-my-traditional-blue.html
**Date:** 2022-09-06T12:38:00-05:00
**Category:** general
**Tags:**

- When a flotilla overlaps or is overlapped by another ship, the other ship only takes a facedown damage card if it is also a flotilla.
- Commanders cannot use flotillas as flagships. They need to use a real ship.
- You cannot have more than 2 flotillas in a fleet.
- If one fleet has all of its non-flotilla ships destroyed, that player loses at the end of the round.
- Keep it cheap. You need to have a very good reason to get it over 30 pts (we will get there)
- Activation padding. Stinks to have to waste 27 pts just for another activation, but your more fragile damage dealing activations will thank you. Looking at you, Patriot Fist. I know it's not exciting, but for a faction with one small base before this, the C-ROC is very helpful in filling out those list archetypes of "I could really use one more activation but I only have about 30 points for it; a Hardcell is just too much!"
- Pushing squads. Pushing 2-3 Vultures isn’t going to eliminate a lot of aces, but it will help. Pushing 2-3 Hyenas, though. THAT could get some work done.
- Token farm. Rune Haako loves silly C-ROCs at distance 5, trodding along tokening commands to feed his hunger.
- Other shenanigans. Wat can use the shields. Commander Grievous can capitalize on a blown up Gozanti. Having another ship on the board to feed off of is beneficial in a lot of builds.
