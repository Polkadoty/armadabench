- Weapon team: Gunnery team - get those 3 attacks in as much as possible. Advanced Gunnery is a tempting objective choice, but I am very skeptical that it's worth giving up the solid Gunnery Team upgrade for.
- Ion Cannon: Like most big ships, this is the "Leading Shots" slot. Vader or Screed may feel otherwise, but always Leading Shots, pretty much.
- Turbolaser: Quad Battery Turrets. You're a big slow ship. Get those extra dice! An argument can be made for XI7s or perhaps H9 Turbolasers, but the QBTs can proc on all 3 attacks, which is pretty sweet.
