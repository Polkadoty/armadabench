# Tournament structure

**Source:** https://cannotgetyourshipout.blogspot.com/2017/07/tournament-structure.html
**Date:** 2017-07-26T23:41:00-05:00
**Category:** general
**Tags:** 

---

- Ship and squadron models
- Ship and squadron cards
- Speed and command dials
- Tokens of all kinds (objective tokens, ship tokens, all kinds of tokens)
- Obstacles
- Damage deck (no sharing with your opponent, this one is for you only)
- Upgrade cards (no proxies!)
- Objective cards
- Attack dice sufficient for your entire fleet (no borrowing mid-game!)
- A range ruler
- A maneuver tool
- Received a bye: That player receives an 8-3 win with a margin of victory of 140 points.
- Opponent's non-flotilla ships completely destroyed at the end of a game round: The player with surviving ships is the winner, regardless of margin of victory. If the winner has a negative margin of victory (this should be very rare normally but could be the case if the losing player was milking an objective like Fire Lanes like crazy), they receive a 6-5 win with a 0 point margin of victory.
- Mutual complete destruction: If at the end of a game round, all players' non-flotilla ships are destroyed, then the second player wins with a margin of victory of 0 (regardless of other points scored from objectives and the like).
- Tiebreaker: if both players tie on the number of points scored (giving both a margin of victory of 0), then the second player is the winner with a margin of victory of 0.
- Conceding: see below!
