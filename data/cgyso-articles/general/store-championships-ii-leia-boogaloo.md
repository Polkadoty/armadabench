So the goal of this (besides having 7 activations) is to try to leverage Leia's ability by dogpiling on whatever I need to dogpile on.  I have a LOT of Leia thoughts, and I'll write up some stories of it afterwards, but I don't want to give my thoughts on Leia too soon until I've played more games with her (today's games will be the 3rd, 4th, and potentially 5th (assuming 3 rounds) with Leia), so I don't want to pretend like I know what I'm doing here yet.

What I'm expecting to face today: all the Avenger Boarding Troopers and several fleets with decent bids.  I haven't played much at Pastimes (where we're playing today) mainly because it's a decent drive away from my apartment (like an hour plus?) but my memories of them involve fleets with 9 plus point bids on average if not more (the guy who won Milwaukee Regionals, Jerry, usually runs with a 15 point bid IIRC) and (Wave 5 and earlier) usually very few squadrons.  I have NO idea what Sloane has done to Pastimes and what I'll be facing.  Who knows, maybe here comes 134 points of Imp squads, a Quasar, and Demo or something? Heck if i know.


While I enjoy "Here They Come", in my opinion "The Asteroid Field" is a superior song (1:18, second link).


Why'd I bring this list?
6 A-wings because I need a squadron presence and I need them to potentially handle whatever I get thrown at me from Sloane and a Quasar and blah blah blah.  Worst case I swing on a ship Wave 1 style and roll a black non-bomber dice. Potentially 6 damage is nothing to sneeze at, and it certainly helps wipe a Star Destroyer's shields. Everything else is helped by Leia's ability (more on that eventually....) and nothing is really crazy screwed over by Avenger Boarding Troopers (if it gets in a Star Destroyer's front arc, it's gonna die anyway, boarding troops or no boarding troops).

But mainly because I wanted to play with Leia.  She seems fun and I have many crazy ideas for working with her.  Given that I got second last time and got everything but the trophy, my goal for today is finish top 8.  I wants me another set of them fancy pants Evade tokens (as a man who runs multiple MC30s, flotillas, CR90s, and such like that (3 hammerheads, say!) I need more fancy evades. Needs them).  Do I want to win it all and get a goofy trophy that I can show off? Yes.  Am I going in with a mostly untested list that I'm playing to enjoy myself and start to learn the intricacies of Leia before the next Regionals season ever drops? More actual yes.
